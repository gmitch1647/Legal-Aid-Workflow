\# FDCPA Knowledge Base — Master Index  
  
\*\*Purpose.\*\* A RAG-ready corpus on the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA), 15 U.S.C. §§ 1692–1692p, and its implementing regulation (CFPB Regulation F, 12 C.F.R. Part 1006). Designed to ground a consumer-law chatbot in primary-source authority — statute citations, agency guidance, and federal case law — rather than copyrighted treatise text.  
  
\*\*Corpus version:\*\* 1.0  
\*\*Last updated:\*\* May 16, 2026  
\*\*File count:\*\* 15 markdown files (this index + 14 substantive files)  
\*\*Total length:\*\* \~43,000 words  
  
\*\*Companion corpora (already complete for FCRA):\*\*  
\- FCRA Knowledge Base  
\- FCRA Discovery Toolkit  
\- FCRA Pleadings & Motion Practice  
  
\---  
  
\#\# How to use this corpus  
  
Each file is a stand-alone topical reference with:  
\- A \*\*statutory citation header\*\* identifying primary § references  
\- \*\*Cross-references\*\* to other files in the corpus, Reg F sections, and case law  
\- \*\*Practical litigation/compliance notes\*\* flagging common issues  
\- \*\*Inline \`\[VERIFY\]\` markers\*\* wherever a subagent could not pin down exact subsection lettering or case citations  
  
For a chatbot, ingest all files together — they cross-reference each other deliberately.  
  
\*\*No content was extracted from copyrighted NCLC treatises or any other paywalled practitioner manuals.\*\* The corpus is built from public-domain statutory and regulatory text, public-domain federal court decisions, and U.S. government agency materials.

\#\# Key SCOTUS cases (quick reference)  
  
| Case | Citation | Holding |  
|------|----------|---------|  
| \*Heintz v. Jenkins\* | 514 U.S. 291 (1995) | Attorneys in consumer-debt litigation are "debt collectors" |  
| \*Jerman v. Carlisle\* | 559 U.S. 573 (2010) | Bona fide error defense does NOT cover mistakes of law |  
| \*Marx v. General Revenue\* | 568 U.S. 371 (2013) | Costs to prevailing defendant under FRCP 54(d) without bad-faith showing |  
| \*Sheriff v. Gillie\* | 578 U.S. 317 (2016) | State AGs are exempt from FDCPA as state actors |  
| \*Midland Funding v. Johnson\* | 581 U.S. 224 (2017) | Filing time-barred POC in bankruptcy is not per se FDCPA violation |  
| \*Henson v. Santander\* | 582 U.S. 79 (2017) | Defaulted-debt purchaser collecting for itself is not a "debt collector" |  
| \*Obduskey v. McCarthy\* | 586 U.S. 466 (2019) | Non-judicial-foreclosure firm not a "debt collector" for most FDCPA provisions |  
| \*Rotkiske v. Klemm\* | 589 U.S. 8 (2019) | 1-year SOL runs from violation, not discovery |

\#\# What's NOT in this corpus (yet)  
  
\- \*\*Verbatim statutory text.\*\* Analytical files cover every provision with short verified quotations but not full verbatim § text. The FTC publishes a clean PDF of the FDCPA at ftc.gov; drop it into the workspace and Claude can integrate.  
\- \*\*Items flagged \`\[VERIFY\]\` inline:\*\* A handful of subsection lettering items and case citations are flagged for confirmation before being cited in pleadings.
