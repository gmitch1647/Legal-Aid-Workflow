# FDCPA Knowledge Base — Master Index

**Purpose.** This corpus is a RAG-ready reference on the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA), 15 U.S.C. §§ 1692–1692p, and its implementing regulation (CFPB Regulation F, 12 C.F.R. Part 1006). It is designed to ground a consumer-law chatbot in primary-source authority.
