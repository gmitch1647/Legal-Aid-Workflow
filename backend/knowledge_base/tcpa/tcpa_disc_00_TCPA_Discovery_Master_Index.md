# TCPA Discovery Toolkit — Master Index  
  
**Purpose.** A RAG-ready corpus on TCPA discovery practice for a consumer-law chatbot. Built from FRCP, TCPA (47 U.S.C. § 227), FCC rules (47 C.F.R. § 64.1200), FCC declaratory rulings and reports & orders, and published federal court decisions. Both plaintiff- and defense-side coverage.  
  
**Corpus version:** 1.0  
**Last updated:** May 16, 2026  
**File count:** 25 markdown files (this index + 24 substantive files)  
**Total length:** ~70,000 words  
  
**Companion corpus:** TCPA Knowledge Base (statute, FCC rules, FCC orders, case law, agency guidance).  
  
**Three-folder TCPA corpus structure:** Knowledge Base → Discovery Toolkit (this folder) → Pleadings & Motion Practice (next).  
  
---  
  
## How to use this corpus  
  
Each file is keyed to a specific discovery task. Sample interrogatories, RFPs, RFAs, 30(b)(6) topics, deposition outlines, and motion language are clearly marked as **templates to adapt**. Every sample is annotated with (a) the TCPA provision, FCC rule, or doctrine that motivates it, (b) the case law that supports it, and (c) common opposing objections.  
  
**No content was extracted from copyrighted NCLC treatises or any other paywalled practitioner manuals.** The corpus is built from public-domain primary sources.

## What this corpus is and isn't  
  
**Is:** A starting reference for TCPA discovery practice — sample templates, strategic guidance, case-law support, and defendant-specific playbooks — ingestible into a RAG pipeline.  
  
**Isn't:** A substitute for jurisdiction-specific local rules research, case-specific scoping, or human attorney judgment. Citations must be Shepardized.  
  
**Items flagged `[VERIFY]` or `[VERIFY CITATION]` inline:** A handful of citations (including FCC order paragraph numbers, certain district-court WL cites, and defendant-specific facts in file 24) are flagged for confirmation before pleading citation.
