\# FDCPA Discovery Toolkit — Master Index  
  
\*\*Purpose.\*\* A RAG-ready corpus on FDCPA discovery practice for a consumer-law chatbot. Built from FRCP, FDCPA (15 U.S.C. §§ 1692 et seq.), Regulation F (12 C.F.R. Part 1006), published federal court opinions, and CFPB/FTC public guidance. Both plaintiff- and defense-side coverage.  
  
\*\*Corpus version:\*\* 1.0  
\*\*Last updated:\*\* May 16, 2026  
\*\*File count:\*\* 25 markdown files (this index + 24 substantive files)  
\*\*Total length:\*\* \~64,000 words  
  
\*\*Companion corpus:\*\* FDCPA Knowledge Base (statute, regulation, case law, agency guidance).  
  
\*\*Three-folder FDCPA corpus structure:\*\* Knowledge Base → Discovery Toolkit (this folder) → Pleadings & Motion Practice (next).  
  
\---  
  
\#\# How to use this corpus  
  
Each file is keyed to a specific discovery task. Sample interrogatories, RFPs, RFAs, 30(b)(6) topics, deposition outlines, and motion language are clearly marked as \*\*templates to adapt\*\*. Every sample is annotated with (a) the FDCPA provision or doctrine that motivates it, (b) the case law that supports it, and (c) common opposing objections.  
  
\*\*No content was extracted from copyrighted NCLC treatises or any other paywalled practitioner manuals.\*\* The corpus is built from public-domain primary sources.

\#\# What this corpus is and isn't  
  
\*\*Is:\*\* A starting reference for FDCPA discovery practice — sample templates, strategic guidance, case-law support, and defendant-specific playbooks — ingestible into a RAG pipeline.  
  
\*\*Isn't:\*\* A substitute for jurisdiction-specific local rules research, case-specific scoping, or human attorney judgment. Citations must be Shepardized.  
  
\*\*Items flagged \`\[VERIFY\]\` inline:\*\* A handful of citations and defendant-specific facts are flagged for confirmation before pleading citation. See file 24 in particular.
