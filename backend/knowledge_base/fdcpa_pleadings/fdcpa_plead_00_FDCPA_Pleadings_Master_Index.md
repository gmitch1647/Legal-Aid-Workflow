# FDCPA Pleadings & Motion Practice — Master Index  
  
**Purpose.** A RAG-ready corpus on FDCPA pleadings and pretrial motion practice for a consumer-law chatbot. Built from FRCP, FRE, FDCPA (15 U.S.C. §§ 1692 et seq.), Regulation F (12 C.F.R. Part 1006), published federal court opinions, and CFPB/FTC public guidance. Both plaintiff- and defense-side coverage.  
  
**Corpus version:** 1.0  
**Last updated:** May 16, 2026  
**File count:** 16 markdown files (this index + 15 substantive files)  
**Total length:** ~47,000 words  
  
**Three-folder FDCPA corpus complete:**  
1. FDCPA Knowledge Base — 15 files, ~43,000 words  
2. FDCPA Discovery Toolkit — 25 files, ~64,000 words  
3. **FDCPA Pleadings & Motion Practice — this folder, 16 files, ~47,000 words**  
  
**Grand FDCPA total: 56 files, ~154,000 words**  
  
---  
  
## How to use this corpus  
  
Each file is keyed to a specific pleading or motion task. Sample briefs, complaint paragraphs, and motion language are clearly marked as **templates to adapt**. Every sample is annotated with (a) the FDCPA provision or doctrine that motivates it, (b) the case law that supports the argument, and (c) common opposing counterarguments.  
  
**No content was extracted from copyrighted NCLC treatises or any other paywalled practitioner manuals.** The corpus is built from public-domain primary sources.

## What this corpus is and isn't  
  
**Is:** A starting reference for FDCPA pleadings and pretrial motion practice — sample briefs, complaint templates, strategic guidance, case-law support.  
  
**Isn't:** A substitute for jurisdiction-specific local rules research, case-specific scoping, or human attorney judgment. Citations must be Shepardized.  
  
**Items flagged `[VERIFY CITATION]`:** A few citations in file 15 are flagged where exact reporter cites or circuit-by-circuit application required confirmation. *Maddox v. BNYM* in particular has both a May 2021 (997 F.3d 436) and November 2021 (19 F.4th 58) opinion; the post-*TransUnion* November opinion is the standing-relevant one.
